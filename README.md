# PolitiTilt
A tool to judge the political leaning (left or right) of Indian political debaters scientifically.

## Overview
PolitiTilt analyzes data from Twitter (X) hashtags to place political debaters, influencers, and trending conversations on a political spectrum. Designed for both Web and Mobile platforms, it empowers the general public to understand the political biases and echo chambers surrounding Indian political discourse.

## Core Features

### 1. Spectrum Visualization
* **Dynamic Political Spectrum:** Instead of a simple "Left vs. Right" binary, the app places individuals and hashtags on a continuous spectrum, reflecting the complex nuances of Indian politics.
* **Hashtag Tracking:** Users can search for trending Twitter hashtags to see the aggregate political leaning of the conversation and identify the most influential voices driving the narrative.

### 2. Scientific NLP & Stance Detection
* **Policy-Based Sentiment Analysis:** Evaluates tweets against key Indian political themes (e.g., economy, religion, welfare, national security) using Natural Language Processing (NLP).
* **Echo Chamber Mapping:** Generates network graphs showing how users interact, retweet, and cluster together, identifying isolated echo chambers versus cross-aisle engagement.
* **Stance Detection Models:** Uses machine learning models trained on Indian political manifestos and historical speeches to scientifically detect alignment with specific ideologies.

### 3. Multilingual Support
* **Vernacular Language Processing:** Given the linguistic diversity in India, the app supports analysis of tweets in Hindi, English, and major regional languages (e.g., Bengali, Tamil, Marathi, Telugu) to capture debates happening outside the English-speaking bubble.
* **Code-Switching Recognition:** Handles "Hinglish" and other mixed-language text commonly used on Indian social media.

### 4. Cross-Platform Accessibility (Web & Mobile)
* **Mobile App (iOS & Android):** Push notifications for shifts in trending hashtags, easily shareable infographics for social media, and quick spectrum checks.
* **Web Dashboard:** Deep-dive analytics, interactive network graphs, and detailed historical tracking of a debater's stance over time.

### 5. Transparency & Education for the General Public
* **"Why This Score?" Feature:** A breakdown for the general public explaining *why* a certain debater or hashtag received its score, highlighting the specific words, policies, or network connections that influenced the result.
* **Bias Alerts:** Flags highly polarized conversations and suggests neutral or opposing viewpoints to encourage a well-rounded understanding of the issue.
